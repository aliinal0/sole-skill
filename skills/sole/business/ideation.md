# Ideation - new solutions, products and growth ideas

Goal: original, testable ideas, not the ten things every AI lists first.

## 1. Frame the problem

Settle with `core/intent.md`: whose problem, how they solve it today, what it costs them, constraints (budget, team, time, skills), and what a win looks like in numbers.

## 2. Get facts first

Send `researcher` agents in parallel for: how people solve it today (forums, reviews, Reddit), existing products, and adjacent industries that solved a similar problem. Ideas built on facts beat ideas built on vibes.

No research tools, or no time for research? Say so in one line, and phrase every local or market claim as an assumption the user can check ("if students sell bikes cheaply in June..."), never as a fact.

## 3. Generate wide (use several lenses)

Produce 3-5 ideas per lens, then merge:
- **Inversion**: what would make the problem worse? Reverse it.
- **Remove a step**: which part of today's workflow could disappear?
- **Borrow from another industry**: who solved this shape of problem elsewhere?
- **Constraint flip**: what if budget were 10x smaller, or time 10x shorter?
- **Unbundle / bundle**: split one big product into a sharp tool, or combine three tools.
- **Who is underserved**: beginners, experts, a region, a language, a niche profession.
- **Distribution first**: where are the users already? Build the idea that fits that channel.

Kill the first idea that comes to mind if it is the obvious one; it is usually what competitors already did.

## 4. Score

Launch the `strategist` agent (or score yourself) on each idea, 1-5:

| Idea | Pain strength | Reach | Feasibility | Differentiation | Cost to test | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 5. Shape the top 3

For each: one-line pitch, who it is for, the riskiest assumption, the cheapest test of that assumption (landing page, interviews, concierge MVP, ad test), and the metric that says go/stop, with a number ("5 morning drop-offs a week by week 4, or stop").

The answer always shows the score table from step 4 (short is fine) and the top 3 in this shape. A list of ideas without scores or go/stop numbers is not finished.

## Growth idea bank (pick by stage and budget)

Low budget: founder-led content, community answers, free tool, templates, integrations listings, directories, partnerships, referral loop.
Leads fast: targeted outbound, paid search on high-intent terms, webinars with partners.
Authority: original data report, open-source piece, teardown series.
Product-led: free tier with a clear upgrade trigger, shareable output, onboarding that reaches value in minutes.
